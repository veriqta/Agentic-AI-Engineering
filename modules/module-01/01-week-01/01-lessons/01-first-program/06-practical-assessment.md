# 06. Practical Assessment: Locate and diagnose a source file

## Your task

Create your first_agent.py from the lab in your practice directory. Run it successfully. Then, without deleting or changing it, attempt to run a filename that does not exist, such as missing_first_agent.py. Record the actual error. Use pwd and ls to identify the current location and real filename. Rerun the correct command and explain why this was a file-selection failure rather than a defect in print.

## Evidence and review

Keep the exact source revision, commands, actual output and your explanation of the responsible statements. Label expected results separately from actual execution. If the result differs from your prediction, investigate the difference and verify a correction. Do not copy a reference result into your own evidence.

## Criteria

Your evidence shows the successful output, the file-not-found error, the inspected location and the corrected run. You can explain the shell, interpreter and source-file roles. You identify which operation selected the wrong path. You do not replace your working source to solve a path mistake.

Record meets criteria, needs revision or not yet assessed for each requirement. You are not expected to memorize the script. You must be able to locate the relevant code and explain its behavior. The reference assigns no individual grade.

## Progression

Use the knowledge check and practical result together before the next major dependency. This assessment verifies one lesson milestone. It does not by itself complete Week 01 or Module 01.
