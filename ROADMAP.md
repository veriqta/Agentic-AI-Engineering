# 52-Week Agentic AI Engineering Roadmap

**Format:** 13 modules, 4 weeks each. Each week has instruction, a hands-on lab, debugging and a verified project increment. Actual progress depends on completion, not calendar dates.

## Module 01: Python & Software Engineering for AI (Weeks 1–4)

**Topics:** Python, APIs, JSON, Git, testing, debugging.

**Portfolio milestone:** Student Support Automation Toolkit.

- Week 1: Understand the problem and build the baseline.
- Week 2: Integrate a meaningful capability.
- Week 3: Test, debug, secure and measure.
- Week 4: Complete the project, document evidence and review.
